### PHASE 3: THE FINAL INVESTIGATIVE REPORT (FIR)

**FINAL INVESTIGATIVE REPORT**
**CASE ID:** FNS-2026-0884 | **TARGET:** Lone Star Independent School District (LSISD)

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**
On July 27, 2026, the TDA F&N Division initiated an investigation into LSISD regarding concurrent allegations of Civil Rights violations (failure to provide LEP accommodations) and NSLP resource management violations. Through procedural review of district communications, physical site documentation, and financial ledgers, the investigation assessed compliance with 7 CFR Part 15 and 7 CFR Part 210. The findings dictate immediate structural remediation regarding LEP household communications, while the financial inquiry remains administratively paralyzed by third-party data latency.

**EVIDENTIARY ANALYSIS**

1. **Civil Rights Records Assessment:** Physical inspection of the LSISD main administrative office and three primary campus intake points confirmed an absolute absence of Spanish-translated NSLP applications. Furthermore, Respondent testimony confirmed a known procurement failure with their printing vendor dating back to August, with no emergency contingency implemented. The complainant's email (Exhibit C-1) establishes that LSISD leadership was notified of the deficiency and failed to mitigate the communication barrier.
2. **Financial Ledger Audit:** Review of the district’s November general ledger confirms a transfer of $42,500 from the protected nonprofit food service account to the district's general fund. Respondent claims this was an automated misconfiguration by the third-party software vendor, EduLedger.
3. **Witness Credibility:** The Complainant’s testimony was corroborated by physical timestamps and physical site conditions. The Respondent was cooperative but failed to produce the necessary backend IT audit logs from EduLedger to validate the claim of software malfunction, rendering the intent of the financial transfer unverified.

**FORMAL LEGAL DETERMINATIONS**

* **Allegation A (Civil Rights): SUBSTANTIATED.** Evidence conclusively demonstrates LSISD failed to provide reasonable steps to ensure meaningful access to program information for LEP individuals, directly violating 7 CFR § 15.3 and FNS Instruction 113-1.
* **Allegation B (Financial/Programmatic): INCONCLUSIVE.** While a transfer occurred contrary to 7 CFR § 210.14(a), the district's third-party financial software vendor (EduLedger) is formally non-responsive. Without system access logs, TDA cannot establish whether this was a willful diversion of federal funds by the district or an erroneous software execution.

**CORRECTIVE ACTION PROGRAM (CAP)**
To remedy the substantiated Civil Rights breach, LSISD must execute the following time-bound actions:

1. **Mandatory Reprint and Distribution:** Within 10 business days of this report, LSISD must physically supply all campus offices with USDA-approved Spanish translations of the NSLP intake forms and distribute bilingual monthly menus.
2. **LEP Contingency Protocol:** Within 30 days, LSISD must draft and submit a formal Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) to TDA detailing the digital and physical contingency plans for LEP communication in the event of future vendor supply chain failures.
3. **Staff Civil Rights Retraining:** Within 45 days, all LSISD front-office personnel and nutrition administrators must complete a TDA-certified Civil Rights and LEP compliance training module, submitting the official roster of completion to the Office of General Counsel.
